Controlling customer behavior, material rules, and default priorities.

Account Group Controls & Units of Measure

The Account Group controls how customer master screens behave. Fields can be set to: Suppress, Display, Optional, or Required. This guarantees data quality across various partner types.

Units of Measure Hierarchy Example
Base Unit
1 Piece

Fundamental unit for stock valuation & inventory balance.

Sales Unit
1 Box (= 5 Pieces)

Proposed default unit during order entry.

Delivery Unit
Multiples of 30 Pcs

Restricts delivery creation to exact pallet/crate multiples.

Customer-Material Information Record & Master Data Priority

When SAP looks for delivering plants, delivery priorities, or customer-specific material identifiers, it follows a strict 3-tier priority sequence:

1. Cust-Mat Info Record (VD51)
2. Customer Master (VD01/XD01)
3. Material Master (MM01)

* Example: Customer-specific plant defaults in VD51 will always override general plant defaults in the Material Master!
